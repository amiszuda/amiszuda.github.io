Hi, my name is Amadeusz Miszuda and I am an astrophysicist from Poland. My work concentrates on variuos aspects of stellar astrophysics connected with binary systems and asterosiesmology - a study of stellar oscillations. 

Currently, I am a post-doctoral researcher at the Nicolaus Copernicus Astronomical Center of the Polish Academy of Sciences in Warsaw, Poland. 
I earned my PhD in Astronomy from the University of Wroclaw in 2023, where I focused on the evolution and pulsations of β Cephei and δ Scuti stars in eclipsing binary systems under the supervision of Prof. Jadwiga Daszyńska-Daszkiewicz.

If you want to reach out, please do so at [amiszuda.astro@gmail.com](mailto:amiszuda.astro@gmail.com)
